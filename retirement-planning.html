Retirement Planning

Preparing for your future

Am I on track to retire? How much should I be investing now? How do I balance how much I am investing vs how much I need now to live? BRG is here to help you make sense of all this.

Your portfolio should grow with you

The right portfolio at 30 is not the right portfolio at 60. We match risk and return to the stage you are actually in.

  • Accumulation — prioritize long term growth as your time horizon allows you to tolerate greater market volatility.
  • Consolidation — balance continued growth with the increasing importance of preserving accumulated wealth.
  • Pre-retirement — reassess portfolio risk, liquidity, and income needs as retirement becomes within reach.
  • Retirement — transition from maximizing accumulation to managing withdrawals, longevity, taxes, and sustainable spending.

The process

Building Towards the Golden Years

  • Plan — define what financial independence looks like.
  • Allocate — align risk and return characteristics with your goals and time horizon.
  • Optimize — invest in a portfolio that is suitable to your situation.
  • Review — annually monitor results and adjust as circumstances change.

Answering important questions

Retirement Planning FAQs

How do you know if I am on track to retire?

BRG starts by understanding your current spending, savings, investments, and goals. This information allows us to make projections about your future assets. From there we use scenario analysis and run simulations to determine solvency levels at age 90, 95, and 100. These numbers are based on your current risk and return levels and can be adjusted for different cycles of your life.

How much should I be investing now?

There isn’t a universal savings rate. It depends on the retirement you are trying to create. What expenses do you envision yourself having throughout retirement, and is it the same, more, or less than your current lifestyle? Building a margin of safety by overestimating is something BRG does. We can help make sense of this to determine how much makes sense to contribute today.

How should my investment strategy evolve as I get closer to retirement?

As retirement approaches, your investment strategy should reflect changes in factors such as risk tolerance, risk capacity, time horizon, and liquidity needs. In an ideal scenario, you have enough in retirement savings to take risk off. However, if your goal is to retire sooner, you may need to target higher growth and potentially greater investment risk.

How do I incorporate tax planning into my retirement?

Tax planning is a key part of retirement planning. Diversifying your retirement assets between traditional and Roth accounts gives flexibility. BRG guides clients in tax planning by evaluating planning around contributions, conversions, withdrawals, Social Security, and estate strategies. Our goal is to improve the after tax outcome of your plan and what you may be potentially leaving to heirs.
