Answers

Frequently asked questions

Straightforward answers to what people ask most. Still curious? A quick call is the fastest way to a real answer.

What does it cost to work with BRG Financial?

We’re fee-based and transparent — investment management is typically a percentage of assets we manage for you, always under a fiduciary commitment we’ll put in writing. No hidden commissions. We’ll walk through exact numbers on an intro call.

Are you a fiduciary?

Yes, 100% of the time. As a Registered Investment Adviser we’re legally required to act in your best interest.

Is there an account minimum?

We work with clients at a range of stages. The best first step is a short, no-cost call to see where you stand and whether we’re a fit.

What states can you serve clients in?

We’re registered in Pennsylvania and New Jersey and can work with clients in several other states on request. Just ask and we’ll confirm your state.

Should I roll over my old 401(k)?

Sometimes yes, sometimes no — it depends on fees, options, taxes, and your goals. We’ll review the specifics before recommending anything. See our 401(k) & ERISA services.

What’s the difference between Roth and traditional?

Traditional is generally pre-tax now and taxed at withdrawal; Roth is after-tax now and tax-free later. Which wins depends on your tax rate today versus in retirement. We break it down in this article.

Do you offer a formal written financial plan?

We do the planning work — projections, tax strategy, scenario and Monte Carlo modeling — as an integrated part of managing your money, tailored to you, rather than as a separate written-plan product.

How is my money actually invested?

In diversified, low-cost building blocks, assembled with intent — growth tilts, alternatives, and optional individual positions — to be efficient for your risk level. More on our investment approach.
