About

An independent adviser in your corner

Why BRG Financial exists

BRG Financial, LLC is solely owned and operated by Benjamin Gialloreto, CFA. A former student-athlete at Immaculata University, Ben pursued a dual major in Finance and Business Management, graduating with Summa Cum Laude honors in 2018. Fueled by a passion for investments, he entered the financial industry immediately after college and earned the CFA Charter in 2022. In the fall of 2023, he founded BRG Financial, an asset management firm headquartered in Pennsylvania.

BRG’s mission is to bring forth personalized investment solutions tailored to clients’ personal goals. We prioritize building enduring, impactful relationships and recognize the unique needs of each household. Our investment philosophy is centered around efficiency — the idea of seeking the strongest returns for the level of risk you are willing and able to take.

Business aside, Ben loves spending time with family and friends — ideally in Wildwood Crest, NJ, where his family has spent summers for more than 70 years. He’s also a die-hard Philadelphia sports fan who never passes up attending an Eagles or Phillies game.

As a Registered Investment Adviser, BRG is held to a fiduciary standard — legally obligated to act in your best interest, full stop.
